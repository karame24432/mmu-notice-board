You Just Clicked a Random Link.

You received this URL from someone you know — and you clicked it without thinking twice. In a real attack, that's all it takes.

🚨 Why This Is Dangerous

  • 🎣 Phishing links often look exactly like links from friends or classmates
  • 🔐 One click can silently install malware on your device
  • 💳 Your passwords, banking details, and files can be stolen instantly
  • 🔒 Ransomware can lock your entire device and demand payment to unlock it

✅ What You Should Do

  • 🤔 Always ask yourself: am I expecting this link?
  • 🔍 Hover over links to preview the actual URL before clicking
  • 💬 Verify with the sender through a separate message before opening
  • 🛡️ Keep your OS and antivirus software up to date
